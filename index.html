Under The Helmet -- The Football Side

Hi, I’m Matthew Skaza, a Class of 2027 student-athlete and multi-sport player at Chicopee Comprehensive High School. I primarily play Defensive Back and Outside Linebacker, but my versatility allows me to contribute at multiple positions on both sides of the ball during varsity competition.

At 5'11", 163 pounds, I continue to develop as an athlete by getting bigger, stronger, and faster. I compete in both football and indoor/outdoor track, using the speed, physicality, and athleticism I develop in each sport to elevate my performance on the field.

During my 2025-2026 junior season, I recorded 3 interceptions, including a pick-six, 3 tackles for loss, and 1 fumble recovery, while also contributing more than 120 kickoff return yards. I take pride in being a physical defender who can make plays in coverage, attack the run, and finish tackles.

My performance earned me All-Western Massachusetts Second Team honors as well as PVIAC Suburban South First Team All-League recognition.

Heading into my senior season, I was selected as one of five team captains for the 2026-2027 football season. I take that responsibility seriously and strive to lead through preparation, physical play, consistency, and effort.

I’m a versatile, physical defensive back who is always looking to make an impact wherever I’m needed. I bring a competitive mindset to every snap and take pride in making plays when my team needs them most.

They call it Skaza Island!
